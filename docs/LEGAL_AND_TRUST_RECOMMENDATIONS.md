# Legal and trust review

These are review outlines for Invicto’s owners.

| Item | Review scope |
| --- | --- |
| Privacy notice | Confirm email draft behavior, direct email handling, host logs, retention, access and deletion requests, and the responsible contact. Review actual data flows before approving language. |
| Terms | Define the site’s informational purpose, permitted use, applicable terms, and contact route. Confirm whether service agreements govern engagements separately. |
| Accessibility statement | Approve an accessibility contact, response process, review date, and disclosure of known limitations after testing with real devices and assistive technology. |
| Security and trust | Publish only approved information about authorized access, confidentiality, retention, incident escalation, and the due diligence process. Document who can receive the full pack. |
| Decision rights | Verify that service and product pages consistently place final title determinations with the client’s examiner or authorized professional. Review lending and valuation wording with the appropriate owners. |

The form prepares a local email draft. Its visible note asks users to avoid borrower details, documents, passwords, and other confidential information. Review the privacy notice again if lead handling changes.
