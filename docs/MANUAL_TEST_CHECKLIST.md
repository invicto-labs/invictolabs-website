# Manual release checks

- On real iPhone Safari, Android Chrome, and iPad Safari, scroll to the middle and footer, open the menu, rotate the device, then close and verify scroll restoration. Check the safe areas and browser bars.
- With VoiceOver, TalkBack, or NVDA, verify the skip link, menu focus and return, service switcher current item, and Approach tabs. Read the catalogue and expand each FAQ.
- At native 200% and 400% zoom, verify the hamburger, heading clearance, and readable reflow. Repeat with reduced motion and keyboard-only navigation.
- Verify the contact form with synthetic data, a configured mail client, and JavaScript disabled. Confirm the draft content without sending it.
- Check print preview, slow network, fresh direct links to each service and Home anchor, and the correct status of an unknown URL on the release host.
